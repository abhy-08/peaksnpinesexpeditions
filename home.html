What is covered

✓ What’s Included

  • Well-maintained group transportation/bus as per itinerary
  • Confirmed hotel accommodations or resort stays
  • Curated meal plans (Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner as specified in your brochure)
  • Campfire arrangements and outdoor activity setups where applicable
  • Experienced tour coordinators and drivers throughout the trip

✕ What’s Excluded

  • Personal expenses like laundry, extra snacks, or bottled beverages outside meal plans
  • Any adventure sports activities or optional boat rides not listed in the package
  • Emergency medical costs or travel insurances
  • Anything not explicitly mentioned under the inclusions list
